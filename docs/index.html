§ 1 — The Central Formula

The Hidden Geometry

Attention has curvature

Every engagement metric you have ever seen — views, likes, watch time, comment rate — is a scalar. It collapses a rich trajectory into a single number, discarding everything interesting about when and how engagement changes.

The right object is not a number but a path: $\mathbf{e}(t) = (r(t),\,\ell(t),\,c(t))$, where $r$ is the retention rate, $\ell$ is the like-accumulation rate, and $c$ is the comment rate, all as functions of time within a video.

Attention Curvature

$$\kappa(t) = \frac{\|\mathbf{e}'(t)\times\mathbf{e}''(t)\|}{\|\mathbf{e}'(t)\|^3}$$ The standard curvature of the space curve $t\mapsto\mathbf{e}(t)$. High $\kappa(t)$ at time $t$ = a sharp bend in audience response: a surprise, an emotional peak, a narrative turn.

Total Attention Work

$$W = \int_0^T \kappa(t)\,\|\mathbf{e}'(t)\|\,dt$$ Length-weighted total curvature of the engagement path. Coordinate-free, scale-invariant. Hypothesis: $W$ predicts virality better than any scalar engagement metric.

The intuition: a video with identical average retention to another but with a sharp curvature spike at minute 3 is a fundamentally different cognitive object. One is smooth and forgettable. The other contains a turn — and turns are what people share.

Why this is new

No published engagement model uses path curvature. Standard models (linear regression on engagement scalars, random forests on metadata) are all scalar-to-scalar. Treating engagement as a geometric object — a path in $\mathbb{R}^3$ — and measuring its curvature is the mathematical core of this project.

Collection Pipeline

1
YouTube Data API v3

80 videos × 10 categories = 800 total. Query by category ID + publish date filter. Free, 10,000 units/day. Saves: ID, title, description, duration, view/like/comment counts, thumbnail URL.

2
yt-dlp — audio + keyframes

Download audio stream only (no full video needed). Extract 5 keyframe thumbnails. Run librosa on audio: pitch, tempo, energy, zero-crossing rate, MFCC. Run Pillow on thumbnails: colour palette, brightness, saturation.

3
Text feature extraction

spaCy for NLP on title + description. textstat for readability score. VADER for sentiment polarity. Custom rules: question mark in title, all-caps words, number of hashtags.

4
Trajectory construction

Re-query each video at days 0, 7, 30, 90 after upload. Compute engagement score at each snapshot. Interpolate to get the path $\mathbf{e}(t)$. Compute path signature (depth 4) via iisignature.

5
Modality classification

Zero-shot CLIP on keyframes: classify as talking-head / screencast / animation / outdoor / music video. This gives the modality flag features for the sheaf-theoretic layer.

§ 3 — Topological Data Analysis

Persistent Homology

The shape of the engagement space

Once you have path signatures for 800 videos, treat them as a point cloud in feature space. Persistent homology reads the topological structure of this cloud across all scales simultaneously — no arbitrary thresholds.

Engagement Persistence Diagram

For each video, treat the retention curve $r:[0,T]\to[0,1]$ as a function and compute its sublevel-set persistent homology. The resulting barcode encodes how many local minima (attention dips) exist, at what depth, and for how long. The persistence entropy $$H_{pers} = -\sum_i \frac{\ell_i}{L}\log\frac{\ell_i}{L},\quad L=\sum_i\ell_i$$ collapses this to a scalar measuring topological complexity of the retention curve.

A video with a single smooth drop has near-zero $H_{pers}$. A video with three sharp dips and recoveries has high $H_{pers}$. The hypothesis: $H_{pers}$ is a better predictor of comment engagement than any content-level feature, because complex retention trajectories are cognitively activating — they keep the brain switching modes.

Mapper graph

The Mapper algorithm (kepler-mapper) projects the 800-video point cloud onto a graph where nodes = clusters of similar videos and edges = shared videos. This visualises the topology of content space: the bridges between comedy and science, the isolated island of philosophy, the dense core of beauty tutorials.

§ 4 — Category Theory & Sheaf Coherence

Green arrows = coherent engagement (audio, visual, text agree).
Red = coherence defect (modalities give conflicting signals).
The $H^1$ obstruction measures total incoherence.

Category-theoretic structure

Engagement as a sheaf over modality space

The four content modalities — visual $V$, auditory $A$, textual $T$, structural $S$ — form a partially ordered set (poset) $\mathcal{M}$. Morphisms are synchronisation maps: how much does the visual rhythm match the audio rhythm? How much does speech prosody match text sentiment?

Content as a functor

Each video $X$ defines a functor $F_X:\mathcal{M}\to\mathbf{Meas}$ sending each modality to the distribution of engagement conditioned on that modality's features. The aggregate engagement should be the colimit of these distributions. When it isn't, there is a coherence defect — a measurable failure of the natural transformation to commute.

Cohomological obstruction

In sheaf-theoretic language, the engagement is a sheaf $\mathcal{E}_X$ over $\mathcal{M}$, and the coherence defect is $$\delta_X = \dim H^1(\mathcal{M},\,\mathcal{E}_X).$$ Hypothesis: low $\delta_X$ predicts stable long-term engagement; high $\delta_X$ predicts volatile but potentially viral content.

This connects directly to recent work by Curry et al. (2025) on stratified spaces in RL agent latent representations, and to the author's preprint on Whitney stratifications of gradient flow closures. The volume growth transform that detects strata in neural networks applies here: engagement trajectories form a stratified space, and the Morse indices govern the volume growth exponents.

§ 5 — Modelling Strategy

Five layers of increasing mathematical depth

Explanation first, prediction second

The modelling sequence is designed to show what each mathematical layer adds. Every model is compared against the previous one.

M1
Baseline scalar model

Predict like-rate from duration + category. Linear regression. This is what everyone does. Establishes the floor.

M2
Full feature model

Add all extracted features: audio, visual, text, structural. LASSO for variable selection. Which features matter? This is the interpretability result — the feature importance story.

M3
Path signature model

Replace engagement scalars with path signature features (depth-4 truncation via iisignature). Does trajectory shape outperform scalar summaries? Hypothesis: yes, because $W$ captures what scalars miss.

M4
Topological model

Add persistence entropy $H_{pers}$ and Betti numbers from the retention curve barcode. Does topological complexity improve prediction beyond the path signature? This is the TDA contribution.

M5
Trajectory VAE (stretch goal)

Train a variational autoencoder on (content features → engagement trajectory) pairs. Sample from the latent space conditioned on content to generate predicted trajectories — not just scalar outputs. This is the deep learning contribution and produces the most visually compelling results.

Model comparison — simulated R² by feature group

Expected finding: path signatures (M3) substantially outperform scalars (M2).
Topological features (M4) add a further lift, especially for comment prediction.

§ 6 — Research Roadmap

Timeline

Six phases, 14 weeks

Ⅰ
Data collection · weeks 1–3

YouTube API pipeline. 800 videos across 10 categories. Audio/visual/text feature extraction. Four-snapshot engagement trajectories.

Ⅱ
Curvature analysis · weeks 3–5

Compute $\kappa(t)$ and $W$ for all videos. Test the hypothesis: does $W$ predict viral status? Identify content features associated with curvature peaks.

Ⅲ
Path signatures · weeks 5–6

iisignature depth-4 features. Compare M1–M3 on held-out test set. Interpretability analysis: which signature coordinates matter?

Ⅳ
Topological layer · weeks 6–8

ripser on retention curves. Mapper graph of full content space. Test $H_{pers}$ as predictor. Visualise the topology of each content category.

Ⅴ
Sheaf coherence · weekss 8–10

Build the modality poset. Estimate synchronisation maps. Compute $\delta_X$ for each video. Test coherence-defect vs engagement-stability hypothesis.

Ⅵ
Trajectory VAE + paper · weeks 10–14

Train generative model. Write up findings. Target: workshop at NeurIPS/ICML (ML track) or journal in computational cognitive science.

Open conjecture

The engagement trajectory $\mathbf{e}(t)$ of a video, viewed as a point cloud in $\mathbb{R}^3$ via Takens delay embedding, forms a Whitney stratified space whose volume growth exponents are controlled by the Morse indices of the retention function $r(t)$.
